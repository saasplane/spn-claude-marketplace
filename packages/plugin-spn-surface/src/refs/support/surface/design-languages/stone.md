# Stone

A dark, calm ground for engineers. Hairlines part the sections, so the page reads as one run of rules and rooms. The one highlight is a stage with a lit edge, and the hero and the closing call take the gradient band. Little moves: a quiet fade as a section arrives.

Sample: the landing page of a developer product in its own brand, dark, at a desktop and a phone. Hero over a soft glow, the product screen framed and raised, points under it, a lit highlight on the one claim, the closing call on the gradient band. Recoloured by the app's theme through the primary token.

Looks: the app's theme values other than the brand, so the brand's primary colour, greys and typefaces stay the platform's.

- **Mode `DARK`, size `MD`, motion `DEFAULT`, radius `MEDIUM`**: the language is a dark, calm ground, so it is dark with the shipped corner and speed, and a size above the dense `SM` for a reader who weighs rather than scans.
- **Layout body `BASE` at `WIDE`; navigation `BASE`, bordered, not raised, entry `LINE`; content `BASE`, no border, no shadow**: hairlines part the sections, so the bars take a line and no shadow.
- **Frames `CONTENT` and `FOOTER`**: a panel holds its words and its foot, and no head, to keep the page one run of rules and rooms.
- **Card `BASE`, bordered, rounded, not raised**: the card is drawn by its edge and never by a shadow, as the sections are.
- **Table not striped, rows hoverable**: a calm table is read by its lines, and the row under the pointer answers it.
