# Daylight

A light, warm ground. Sections part by their own fill and by room, with no lines. The highlight is open: the section stands larger with a backdrop of dots behind it and no frame. Sections rise gently as they arrive, and photos stand flush and rounded.

Sample: the same landing page in its own brand, light, at a desktop and a phone. Hero beside a photo, points beside the product screen, one open highlight on the claim that leads, the closing call on a soft tint. Recoloured by the app's theme through the primary token.

Data: `daylight.json`.
