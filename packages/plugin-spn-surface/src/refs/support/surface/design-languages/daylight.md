# Daylight

A light, warm ground. Sections part by their own fill and by room, with no lines. The highlight is open: the section stands larger with a backdrop of dots behind it and no frame. Sections rise gently as they arrive, and photos stand flush and rounded.

Sample: the same landing page in its own brand, light, at a desktop and a phone. Hero beside a photo, points beside the product screen, one open highlight on the claim that leads, the closing call on a soft tint. Recoloured by the app's theme through the primary token.

Looks: the app's theme values other than the brand, so the brand's primary colour, greys and typefaces stay the platform's.

- **Mode `LIGHT`, size `MD`, motion `SLOW`, radius `LARGE`**: a light, warm ground with soft frames, so the corners are the largest, and the motion the gentlest, as sections rise as they arrive.
- **Layout body `BASE` at `WIDE`; navigation `DEFAULT`, not bordered, not raised, entry `SUBTLE`; content `BASE`, no border, no shadow**: sections part by their own fill and by room with no lines, so the bar takes a fill and no line.
- **Frames `CONTENT` and `FOOTER`**: a panel stands with its words and its foot, and the head stays out for more room.
- **Card `BASE`, raised, rounded, not bordered**: a card lifts off the page by its shadow, as a door lifts under the pointer.
- **Table striped, rows hoverable**: the bands carry the rows with no lines between them.
