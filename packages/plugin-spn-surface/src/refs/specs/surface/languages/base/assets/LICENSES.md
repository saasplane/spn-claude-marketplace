# Pictures of the base language: sources and licences

`For: Web developer` · `Status: ✅ DONE`

- **The illustrations, covers, banners, subject pictures, drawings, logos and wide logos were drawn for this language** on 2026-11-28 as SVG, in flat shapes and one warm palette (cream, sand, terracotta, ochre, sage, teal, plum), following Shared → Drawing. Every company in them is invented, and no known brand is drawn. They belong to this repository.
- **The faces in `pictures/people/` and `pictures/reviews/` are DiceBear "Notionists" by Zoish** (https://bio.link/heyzoish), licensed CC0 1.0 (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/), as read at https://www.dicebear.com/styles/notionists/ on 2026-11-20. They come from `spn-platform-ts/tests/assets/demo-media/avatars/` (see its `LICENSES.md`), each on a tinted ground. CC0 asks for no attribution, and credit is given here anyway.
- **No stock photograph is used.**
