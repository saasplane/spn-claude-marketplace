# Behaviors — Refs

`For: UX designer · Architect` · `Status: 🔮 PLANNING`

The table below lists the promises [Refs](../../02-constructs/02-surface/05-refs.md) makes. A row says what somebody can do and what they see when they do it.

Every row below is declared and none is claimed: a run writes the last two cells.

| Id | Who | Does | Sees | Type | Tier | Status | Updated at |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| MKT.REFS.25 | Partner / integrator | read a Surface rule with no copy of the book beside them | Every Surface ref is a self-contained leaf, written out rather than linked to | POSITIVE | UNIT | PLANNED | — |
| MKT.REFS.26 | Web developer | start a screen or a section from a spec that is the book's own | The spec folders are byte-identical to the book's, and an index lists each with the hash of its contents | POSITIVE | UNIT | PLANNED | — |
