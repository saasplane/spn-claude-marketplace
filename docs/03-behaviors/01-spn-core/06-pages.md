# Behaviors — The Page

`For: Architect · Editor` · `Status: 🔮 PLANNING`

The table below lists the promises [The Page](../../02-constructs/01-spn-core/06-pages.md) makes. A row says what somebody can do and what they see when they do it.

Every row below is declared and none is claimed: a run writes the last two cells.

| Id | Who | Does | Sees | Type | Tier | Status | Updated at |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| MKT.PAGE.01 | Editor | write markdown and never HTML | The page is produced from the seat file, header and furniture included | POSITIVE | UNIT | PLANNED | — |
| MKT.PAGE.02 | Architect | have a hand edit to a page found rather than inherited | The audit produces the page again and refuses any difference, naming the seat file to edit | NEGATIVE | UNIT | PLANNED | — |
| MKT.PAGE.03 | Partner / integrator | read a produced page with nothing installed | The renderer covers the seat file's closed grammar and pulls in no dependency | POSITIVE | UNIT | PLANNED | — |
| MKT.PAGE.04 | Editor | draw a figure by describing it rather than by placing it | Every box is measured from its own text, so a label fits and a connector lands on an edge | POSITIVE | UNIT | PLANNED | — |
| MKT.PAGE.05 | Editor | be told when a figure claims two things touch and they do not | The checker reads the drawn result: labels, connector ends, visible length and clear air | NEGATIVE | UNIT | PLANNED | — |
| MKT.PAGE.06 | Architect | follow a link on a produced page and land where the seat file meant | Each relative link is re-expressed against the page's own folder as it is written | POSITIVE | UNIT | PLANNED | — |
| MKT.PAGE.07 | Quality engineer | read a page's behaviour rows without them being typed into it | The rows are joined from the register beside the construct, and the seat file is not touched | POSITIVE | UNIT | PLANNED | — |
| MKT.PAGE.08 | Architect | tell a generated region of a face from the prose around it | The region sits between markers naming what writes it | POSITIVE | UNIT | PLANNED | — |
