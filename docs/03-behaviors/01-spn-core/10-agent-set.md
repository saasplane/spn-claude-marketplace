# Behaviors — The Agent

`For: Engineering leader · Architect` · `Status: 🔮 PLANNING`

The table below lists the promises [The Agent](../../02-constructs/01-spn-core/10-agent-set.md) makes. A row says what somebody can do and what they see when they do it.

Every row below is declared and none is claimed: a run writes the last two cells.

| Id | Who | Does | Sees | Type | Tier | Status | Updated at |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| MKT.AGENT.01 | Engineering leader | get a read from a context that did not write the work | A persona is convened mid-turn by name, without a second window being opened | POSITIVE | UNIT | PLANNED | — |
| MKT.AGENT.02 | Architect | check a persona's authority before trusting its finding | The brief states what it may decide, and its frontmatter states what it may call | POSITIVE | UNIT | PLANNED | — |
| MKT.AGENT.03 | DevSecOps / Security | tell a bound permission from a stated intention | A brief declaring tools is bound by that list; a brief declaring none inherits the session's own | POSITIVE | UNIT | PLANNED | — |
| MKT.AGENT.04 | Engineering leader | receive a review that reports rather than one that edits as it reads | Exactly one brief may write, and only over the paragraphs it was handed | NEGATIVE | UNIT | PLANNED | — |
| MKT.AGENT.05 | Architect | review from any viewpoint without a brief per viewpoint | One brief takes a viewpoint name, and its own description lists the names a caller may pass | POSITIVE | UNIT | PLANNED | — |
| MKT.AGENT.06 | Engineering leader | tell a finding from a suggestion | Each brief names where its standard lives, and states no rule of its own | POSITIVE | UNIT | PLANNED | — |
