# Behaviors — Agents

`For: Engineering leader · Architect` · `Status: 🔮 PLANNING`

The table below lists the promises [Agents](../../02-constructs/01-devex/03-agents.md) makes. A row says what somebody can do and what they see when they do it.

Every row below is declared and none is claimed: a run writes the last two cells.

| Id | Who | Does | Sees | Type | Tier | Status | Updated at |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| MKT.AGENTS.01 | Engineering leader | get a read from a context that did not write the work | A persona is convened mid-turn by name, without a second window being opened | POSITIVE | UNIT | PLANNED | — |
| MKT.AGENTS.02 | Architect | check a persona's authority before trusting its finding | The brief states what it may decide, and its frontmatter states what it may call | POSITIVE | UNIT | PLANNED | — |
| MKT.AGENTS.03 | DevSecOps / Security | tell a bound permission from a stated intention | A brief declaring tools is bound by that list; a brief declaring none inherits the session's own | POSITIVE | UNIT | PLANNED | — |
| MKT.AGENTS.04 | Engineering leader | receive a review that reports rather than one that edits as it reads | Exactly one brief may write, and only over the paragraphs it was handed | NEGATIVE | UNIT | PLANNED | — |
| MKT.AGENTS.05 | Architect | review from any viewpoint without a brief per viewpoint | One brief takes a viewpoint name, and its own description lists the names a caller may pass | POSITIVE | UNIT | PLANNED | — |
| MKT.AGENTS.06 | Engineering leader | tell a finding from a suggestion | Each brief names where its standard lives, and states no rule of its own | POSITIVE | UNIT | PLANNED | — |
| MKT.AGENTS.07 | Engineering leader | convene one engineering function's judgment over a change | The viewpoint is named at the call, and its file is read before anything is said | POSITIVE | UNIT | PLANNED | — |
| MKT.AGENTS.08 | Architect | know in advance what a reviewer can refuse on | Each viewpoint file states its one block condition, and marks everything below it as advice | POSITIVE | UNIT | PLANNED | — |
| MKT.AGENTS.09 | Engineering leader | have work stopped on a stated threshold rather than on taste | A finding below the threshold is advice, and the file says so in its own words | NEGATIVE | UNIT | PLANNED | — |
| MKT.AGENTS.10 | Architect | change the review procedure once rather than once per viewpoint | The procedure lives in one brief, and the viewpoint files hold only subject matter | POSITIVE | UNIT | PLANNED | — |
| MKT.AGENTS.11 | Editor | declare a document's audience from the same list reviewers are drawn from | A value outside the closed set is refused, and each value renders as its reader label | NEGATIVE | UNIT | PLANNED | — |
| MKT.AGENTS.12 | Architect | be told when a viewpoint file has fallen behind the book | Each file carries a stamp, and the drift run re-reads what it names | NEGATIVE | UNIT | PLANNED | — |
