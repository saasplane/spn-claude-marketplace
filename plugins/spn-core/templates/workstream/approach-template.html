Open — Q{{n}} · Q{{n}}

Every question you cannot answer alone lives here, never in an arc. Numbers never restart and never repeat. A card is a div.open wrapping an h4 whose id is its number — that is what every check reading this page looks for, what gives an undecided card its amber edge, and what the rail counts to show how many are waiting. A card written any other way is a card no gate can see and no reader can count.

A card assumes no memory of the session that wrote it, because people decide days later. It carries what led to the question, what is true today, and what happens if nothing is decided.
